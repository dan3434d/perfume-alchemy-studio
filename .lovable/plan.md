# Abdulrahman Perfumes — Editorial Luxe Redesign

## Direction
- Apply the selected Editorial Luxe Minimalist direction across the complete customer storefront.
- Use warm ivory, porcelain, charcoal, stone, and restrained brass accents.
- Pair Cormorant Garamond display typography with Work Sans body typography.
- Keep layouts sharp and editorial: thin rules, 4px corners, generous whitespace, strong imagery, and restrained motion.

## Homepage
- Rebuild the first view as the selected asymmetrical editorial composition with the existing perfume image, clear collection and scent-quiz actions, fragrance notes, and price/value details.
- Reorder the page into a streamlined shopping journey: trust signals, bestsellers, audience collections, designer inspirations, craft story, reviews, scent quiz, FAQs, and newsletter.
- Remove repetitive sales sections and decorative treatment that dilute the premium presentation.

## Storefront
- Redesign navigation, announcement banner, footer, product cards, shop filters, and product listing around the same visual system.
- Refine product detail pages into an image-led editorial purchase layout while preserving notes, inspired-by details, quantity, wishlist, bundle offer, craft story, FAQs, and recommendations.
- Restyle cart, checkout, scent discovery, about, contact, and policy pages through shared global rules and focused page updates, without changing checkout or account logic.

## Quality and consistency
- Preserve all existing products, pricing, promotions, payment methods, authentication, recommendations, and admin functionality.
- Add complete route metadata where missing, keep accessible labels and semantic headings, and support reduced motion.
- Validate desktop and mobile layouts, key shopping interactions, and the live build after implementation.
